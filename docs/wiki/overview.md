# Overview

Math Pentathlon is a browser practice edition of educational strategy math games. Students play in four grade-banded divisions; builders inspect the same TypeScript tree that ships to production.

## What you get

- **20 registered games** — names and metadata from `src/core/game-registry.ts`
- **4 divisions** — Grades K–1 through 6–7
- **Live site** — [https://math.pappas.work](https://math.pappas.work) (Cloudflare Pages, deployed from `alpha`)
- **Open workflows** — CI and deploy status on the [repository README](../../README.md)

## What this is not

- Not a private school ledger or family ops surface
- Not a place to invent catalog entries (for example, there is **no Math Relay**)
- Not a substitute for official tournament rules when those live elsewhere
